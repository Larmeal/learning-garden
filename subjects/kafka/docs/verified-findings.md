## What differs from the books (written for Kafka 2.7 with ZooKeeper)

- No ZooKeeper: `process.roles=broker,controller` and the `__cluster_metadata` log.
- `segment.bytes` has a floor of 1 MiB; tests use `internal.segment.bytes`.
- The Java producer defaults changed (`linger.ms=5`, idempotence on, `acks=all`).
- `kafka-topics --describe` shows `Elr` and `LastKnownElr` columns (eligible leader replicas).
- The consumer group protocol of KIP-848 (`group.protocol=consumer`) exists and was run.

## Not run

TLS, SASL and ACLs, `kafka-reassign-partitions`, tiered storage, MirrorMaker 2, a real Schema Registry, Debezium and any connector that is not a file connector, ksqlDB, Kafka Streams beyond `WordCountDemo` (joins, windows, a topology of our own), share groups (KIP-932),
a cluster of more than 3 brokers, brokers on more than one computer, JMX metrics, static membership and cooperative rebalancing, the Kafka source as a whole, and the figures of the books.
