  client:visible
  lang="en"
  q="A regex takes twice as long with every extra letter. With 20 letters it takes about a million steps. What do you expect with 40 letters?"
  options={['About two million steps', 'About a trillion steps (2 to the power 40), which hangs the CPU for a very long time', 'The same, because the regex was compiled']}
  answer={1}
  why="Doubling per letter is 2 to the power n. At 20 letters that is 1,048,576, as in the exercise. At 40 letters it is 2 to the power 40, about 1.1 trillion steps (this number continues the doubling and was not run). The fix that works at once is to limit the length before the regex."

<Question

  q="When is a sliding window better than a fixed window, and what is the price?"
  options={['It is better when an attacker sends around the edge of a window, because there is no edge to use, but it has to keep the times of the recent requests', 'It is always better, and costs nothing', 'A fixed window is always better']}
  answer={0}
  why="At the edge a fixed window lets all 20 through in 6 seconds with a limit of 10 a minute, while a sliding window lets only 10 through. The price is the extra memory and lookup to keep the times of the requests that went by, which the author did not measure."
