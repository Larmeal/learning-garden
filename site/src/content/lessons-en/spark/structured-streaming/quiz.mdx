<Question
  client:visible
  lang="en"
  q="In one round of a micro-batch, in what order does Spark write offsets/N and commits/N?"
  options={['commits/N first, then offsets/N after the run', 'offsets/N before processing, and commits/N after processing is done', 'Both at the same time after the run']}
  answer={1}
  why="offsets/N is a write-ahead log written before the run, so that after a crash in the middle Spark knows the original data range. commits/N is written at the end to say that this batch is complete."

  q="The checkpoint has offsets/1 but no commits/1, and you restart. What happens to the query?"
  options={['It skips batch 1 and starts at batch 2', 'It runs batch 1 again with the same offset range, and the sink may get batch 1 twice', 'It builds a new batch 1 and picks a new data range']}

<Question
  client:visible
  lang="en"
  q="What is the result of groupBy(user).count() in append mode with no watermark?"
  options={['It works and sends the result in every batch', 'AnalysisException saying that output mode append is not supported for aggregations without a watermark', 'It works but sends only the new rows']}
  answer={1}
  why="Without a watermark, Spark has no way to know which group is finished, so it cannot emit rows that will not change again in append mode. Use update or complete instead, or add a watermark."
/>

</Step>

<Question
  client:visible
  lang="en"
  q="The window 12:00 to 12:05 has two events. The watermark delay is 10 minutes. The newest event so far is 12:03. In append mode, when is this window sent out?"
  options={['Immediately, in the batch that receives the events', 'When the watermark passes 12:05, which needs a new event of at least 12:15, and the next batch applies that value', 'After 10 minutes of real clock time']}
  answer={1}
  why="The watermark is the newest event time minus the delay. It is computed at the end of a batch and used in the next batch. In the real run, the event 12:20 made the watermark 12:10, and the next batch (with no new data) emitted the window. It does not depend on the real clock."
/>

</Step>
