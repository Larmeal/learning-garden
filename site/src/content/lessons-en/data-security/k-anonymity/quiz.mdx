## Check yourself

<Question
  client:visible
  lang="en"
  q="A table is k-anonymous with k equal to 5. What does that mean?"
  options={['Everybody in the table has one of 5 different diagnoses', 'For any combination of quasi identifier values in the table there are at least 5 rows with the same values, so an attacker can point at no fewer than 5 rows', 'The data is encrypted with 5 keys']}
  answer={1}
  why="k-anonymity counts the size of the groups of look-alikes by quasi identifier. It has nothing to do with encryption or with how varied the diagnoses are. In the exercise, at 10 year bands and a 3 digit zip, after throwing away 10 rows every group holds 5 or more."
/>

</Step>

<Step kind="question">

<Question
  client:visible
  lang="en"
  q="With the exact birth year and a 5 digit zip, 1,872 of 2,000 rows would have to be suppressed to reach k = 5. What should the team do?"
  options={['Accept losing all the rows', 'Coarsen the age band or the zip so groups get bigger, for example 10 year bands and a 3 digit zip need only 10 rows suppressed, at the price of detail', 'Add columns to make the table more detailed']}
  answer={1}
  why="Suppressing and coarsening trade against each other: the coarser the data, the fewer rows are suppressed, but the larger the average group. The exercise measures it from 1.9 people to 20.8 at 10 years and a 3 digit zip, and 166.7 when only a 2 digit zip is left."
/>

</Step>

<Step kind="question">

<Question
  client:visible
  lang="en"
  q="A group in a k-anonymous table has 10 people and all of them have the same diagnosis. What does an attacker learn by knowing that his target is in this group?"
  options={['Nothing, because the group has ten people', 'The target\'s diagnosis in full, without needing to know which row it is (a homogeneity attack)', 'The name of the target']}
  answer={1}
  why="When everybody in the group has the same value, not knowing which row is the target's does not help. The exercise finds 5 such groups (62 people) in the release at 10 years and a 3 digit zip. The fix is to require variety in the sensitive value in each group (l-diversity), or to move to differential privacy."
/>

</Step>

<Question
  client:visible
  lang="en"
  q="Why does a 20 year age band add nothing over a 10 year band in this exercise?"
  options={['Because 20 years is easier to guess', 'Because the synthetic people were born over only 55 years, and the smallest group is limited by the zip, which a wider age band does not make bigger', 'Because k goes down when the band gets wider']}
  answer={1}
  why="The exercise asserts that the number of rows to suppress at 20 years equals that at 10 years for every zip tested (5, 4 and 3 digits). One kind of coarsening has a limit. When the smallest group is held in place by another variable, coarsening the first one further does not help."
/>
