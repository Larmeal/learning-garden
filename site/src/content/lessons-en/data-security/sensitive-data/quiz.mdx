## Check yourself

<Question
  client:visible
  lang="en"
  q="The column sex has only two values. Why can it still count as a quasi identifier?"
  options={['Because sex is the most sensitive data', 'Because combined with other columns such as zip and birth date it makes groups smaller until one person is left', 'Because with only two values it is easy to guess']}
  answer={1}
  why="A quasi identifier is not defined by whether a column identifies someone on its own, but by whether it splits groups when used with other columns. In the exercise sex alone gives 2 groups, but sex, zip and the full birth date together give 1,996."
/>

</Step>

<Question
  client:visible
  lang="en"
  q="A team drops the columns email and phone, but forgets the free text column notes. What is the result?"
  options={['Nothing, because notes is not a column of customer data', 'The identifiers are still there. The exercise finds phone numbers in 200 rows and e-mails in 106 rows of this column', 'A regex removes them automatically']}
  answer={1}
  why="A free text column is where identifiers hide, because staff type them in by hand. Every column has to be scanned before a release, and a regex can still miss, for example a number written with dashes or spaces that differ from what we defined."
/>

</Step>

  q="With sex, zip and month and year of birth, the exercise counts 1,757 of 2,000 people alone in their group. What does that number mean?"
