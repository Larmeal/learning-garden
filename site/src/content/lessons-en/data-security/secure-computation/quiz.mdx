## Check yourself

<Question
  client:visible
  lang="en"
  q="In the secret sharing lab, if you look from the side of person 2 alone, what do you see?"
  options={['Everybody\'s salary', 'A column of one share per owner, random numbers that say nothing about any salary, and that person\'s own partial sum', 'The total of all the salaries before anybody']}
  answer={1}
  why="A share that one person receives is uniformly random whatever the secret is. The exercise checks that the last digit of share 1 is spread 9% to 11% per digit. Each partial sum is a random number as well. The real total appears only when the partial sums of all three are added."

<Question
  client:visible
  lang="en"
  q="In the secure aggregation exercise, why is the total wrong when client 17 drops out after sending a masked value, although all the other clients sent theirs?"
  options={['Because the value of client 17 is missing', 'Because the masks that client 17 would have cancelled against the masks of the others are missing too. The masks of the 99 who were paired with client 17 are left over and are not cancelled', 'Because the server calculates wrongly']}
  answer={1}
  why="Every mask is added by one side and subtracted by the other. If one side disappears, the other still holds a mask with no partner. Real protocols add a step to recover the masks of those who drop out, which the author did not run, and which is the most complicated part."
/>

</Step>

  q="In PSI, why can company B, which receives the numbers that A raised to a power, not undo them with a dictionary of e-mails?"
