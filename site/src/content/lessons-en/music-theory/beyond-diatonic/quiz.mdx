  q="What separates a secondary dominant from a modulation?"
  options={['A secondary dominant uses seventh chords, a modulation does not', 'A secondary dominant is a short visit to a home, a modulation moves house until the ear accepts a new tonic', 'There is no difference']}
  answer={1}
  why="The difference is the length of time and the new footing. If the new tonic stays long enough and there is a cadence in the new key, it is called a modulation."
