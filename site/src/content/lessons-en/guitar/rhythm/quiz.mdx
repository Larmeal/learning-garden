  answer={1}
  why="The right hand must swing steadily like a clock. A pattern is only a choice of which beats let the pick touch the strings. A rest cell is not a cell where the hand stops."

<Question

  q="When you count '1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &', should the right hand go down or up on the '&'?"
  options={['Down', 'Up', 'It depends on the song']}
  answer={1}
  why="The basic rule: go down on the main beats (the numbers) and up on the off-beats (&), so the hand swings as one clock all the time."

</Step>
