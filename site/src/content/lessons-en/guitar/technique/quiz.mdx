<Question

  client:visible
  q="Even when you play the same note, what is the advantage of alternate picking (down up down up)?"
  options={['The sound is louder', 'The right hand swings with the same rhythm all the time, so you play faster and steadier', 'It reduces buzz from the left hand']}
  answer={1}
  why="Alternating direction keeps the hand moving. The down stroke falls on the main beat and the up stroke on the offbeat, which is the same frame as strumming."
/>

</Step>

  q="Where does the right-hand movement in alternate picking mainly come from?"
  options={['Moving the whole arm from the shoulder', 'Rotating the wrist (like turning a door handle) plus a little forearm', 'Bending the thumb and index finger up and down']}
  answer={1}
  why="The wrist can rotate faster and wastes less energy than moving the whole arm. Bending the fingers is not recommended because it supports less speed."
