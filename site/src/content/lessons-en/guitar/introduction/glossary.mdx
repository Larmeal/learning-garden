## How to use it well

- **Read it when you meet a word you do not know. Do not memorize it.** The words you use often will stay in your memory after you meet them again in several lessons.
- **The link at the end of a term** takes you to the lesson that really teaches the term, not just a short explanation.
- **Words that look alike but are different.** Be careful with these pairs.
  - **bar** (a measure of music) and **barre** (pressing several strings with one finger): they sound similar and mean different things.
  - **slide** (a technique of sliding a finger) and **slide** (a metal tube worn on a finger): the same word in different contexts.
  - **vibrato** (the pitch moves up and down) and **tremolo** (the volume changes quickly): the lever on a guitar that changes the pitch is often called a tremolo bar even though it works like vibrato, so you will find both names (and whammy bar).
  - **chord** (several notes played together) and **arpeggio** (the same notes played one at a time).
  - **scale** (a set of notes in order) and **key** (the center of a song's sound).
