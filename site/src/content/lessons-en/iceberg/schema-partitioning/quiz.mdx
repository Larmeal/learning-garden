  q="The table events is partitioned by days(ts), and every file holds both TH and US countries. How many of the 5 files must a query WHERE country = 'TH' open?"

  answer={1}
  why="The exercise measured 5 files. There are two ways to skip files: the partition value and the min/max statistics in the manifest. Our data mixes TH and US in every file, so the min/max range of country covers TH in every file. If the TH data were together in a few files, the statistics could skip more (this last remark is the author's and was not tried)."
