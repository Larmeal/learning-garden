<Question
  client:visible
  lang="en"
  q="We put a taint on one node, and then create a Pod with a toleration that matches that taint. Which statement is right?"
  options={[
    'The Pod must go to that node only, because a toleration attracts Pods',
    'The Pod may land on that node, but it may land on other nodes as well. A toleration permits, it does not force',
    'The Pod cannot land on that node, because a taint forbids every Pod',
    'Pods already running on that node are evicted at once'
  ]}
  answer={1}
  why="A taint says do not put a Pod here unless it tolerates this tag. A toleration only lets the Pod through the gate, and does not tell it to go there. To force it, add a nodeSelector or affinity. And NoSchedule does not evict Pods that are already there."
/>

</Step>

<Step kind="question">

<Question
  client:visible
  lang="en"
  q="A Pod is stuck in Pending with the message 0/3 nodes are available: 2 Insufficient cpu, 1 node had untolerated taint. Which statement sums up the problem?"
  options={[
    'The Pod container cannot start because the image is wrong',
    'No node can take the Pod. Two have too little CPU for the request and the third has a taint the Pod does not tolerate',
    'The scheduler has not started yet',
    'The Pod has been deleted but is still shown'
  ]}
  answer={1}
  why="A FailedScheduling message says why every node was rejected. The problem is at the placement step and not the container step (there is not even a node to run on). Fix it by lowering the request, adding a node, or adding a toleration."
/>

</Step>

<Step kind="question">

<Question
  client:visible
  lang="en"
  q="We drain a node while a Deployment has 6 Pods and a PodDisruptionBudget with minAvailable 6. What is the result?"
  options={[
    'drain succeeds at once and the Pods move to other nodes',
    'drain cannot evict any Pod, because that would take Ready below 6, so it waits until it times out and fails, until the PDB is changed or removed',
    'Kubernetes deletes the PodDisruptionBudget by itself',
    'Every Pod is deleted at the same time'
  ]}
  answer={1}
  why="A PDB is a promise of how many Pods must stay Ready, and drain respects it. If evicting a Pod would break the promise, it does not evict. In the exercise drain failed, and succeeded at once after the PDB was deleted."
/>

</Step>
