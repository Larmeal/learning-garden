<Question
  client:visible
  lang="en"
  q="A Helm release has revisions 1, 2 and 3, and we roll back to revision 1. What is the result?"
  options={[
    'History is wound back and only revision 1 is left',
    'We get revision 4 with the content of revision 1, and history still moves forward',
    'Revisions 2 and 3 are deleted',
    'Helm refuses, because revision 1 has been superseded'
  ]}
  answer={1}
  why="A rollback does not wind history back. It creates a new revision that copies the old content. In the exercise a rollback to revision 1 gave revision 3 with the content of revision 1, and the history stayed complete."

<Step kind="question">

<Question
  client:visible
  lang="en"
  q="An HPA has a CPU target of 50%. There are 2 Pods now, and the average reading is 100% of the request. With no maximum in the way, how many Pods are wanted?"
  options={[
    '2, because the value is over but nothing needs doing',
    '4, because it is the round up of 2 times 100 divided by 50',
    '1',
    '3'
  ]}
  answer={1}
  why="The number wanted = round up of (current number × use ÷ target) = 2 × 100 ÷ 50 = 4. In the exercise we saw the same formula as 1 × 200 ÷ 50 = 4."
/>

</Step>
