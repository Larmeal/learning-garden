<Question
  client:visible
  lang="en"
  q="The SBOM of one artifact gave different matches on day 1 and day 30 without any new build. Why?"
  options={['Because the bytes of the artifact changed', 'Because the list of vulnerabilities we know grew, so the risk of the same bytes changed without anybody touching the code', 'Because the SBOM expired']}
  answer={1}
  why="An SBOM says what the artifact is made of, not whether it has a vulnerability. The result comes from matching against a list of advisories on the day of the match. In the exercise 1 matched on day 1 and 3 on day 30."
/>

</Step>

<Question
  client:visible
  lang="en"
  q="A gate set to fail only on critical lets a build with only a medium finding through. What is the trade-off?"
  options={['There is no trade-off', 'It lets things pass more easily so the pipeline is not blocked often, but we need a way to follow the low severity findings that passed, or they pile up as debt', 'A gate like this does not work']}
  answer={1}
  why="A threshold that is too strict makes the team stop for small things until it no longer trusts the gate, and one that is too loose lets risky things through. In the exercise the gate at high stopped day 1, the gate at critical let medium pass, and the team must keep the SBOM to follow it."
/>

</Step>
