  q="The counter http_requests_total says 5 requests had status 500. Which of these can this metric not tell us?"

<Step kind="question">

<Question
  client:visible
  lang="en"
  q="A cumulative histogram has le=0.1 equal to 45 and le=0.25 equal to 60. How do we read it?"
  options={['45 requests answered within 0.1 seconds, and 15 answered more slowly than that but within 0.25 seconds', '45 requests answered between 0.1 and 0.25 seconds', 'There are 105 requests in all']}
  answer={0}
  why="The buckets are cumulative (le means less than or equal). The number in le=0.25 already includes those of le=0.1, so the count between two edges is a subtraction (60 minus 45 is 15), and +Inf always equals the total."
/>

</Step>

<Step kind="question">

<Question
  client:visible
  lang="en"
  q="A mean response time of 39 ms looks good, but the 95th percentile lies in the 0.25 bucket. What does this reflect?"
  options={['The mean was calculated wrongly', 'A quarter of the requests are very slow, so the mean hides that slowness', 'Prometheus read a bucket wrongly']}
  answer={1}
  why="15 of 60 requests took 150 ms. Averaged with the 45 very fast ones the mean looks low. The users who got the slow requests feel 150 ms, not 39 ms, so we should look at a histogram or a percentile."
/>

</Step>
