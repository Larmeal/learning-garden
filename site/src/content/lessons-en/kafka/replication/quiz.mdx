  q="The leader has written a record at offset 7, but a follower in the ISR has fetched only up to offset 5. Where is the high watermark, and how far can a consumer read?"
  options={[
    'The high watermark equals the end of the leader, because the leader decides. A consumer can read up to 7',
    'The high watermark follows the slowest copy in the ISR (position 5 or 6), and a consumer reads only what is committed',
    'The high watermark is 0 until every broker has fetched everything',
    'The high watermark has nothing to do with consumers'
  ]}
  answer={1}
  why="The high watermark is the position that every replica of the ISR already has, so it is below the end of the leader when a follower is slow. Consumers can read only records that every copy has, so that a record that might be lost when the leader crashes is never read (book: Kafka: The Definitive Guide, page 140). The lab of this lesson shows the cells that are written but not yet committed."

    'In the normal stop, because the broker has to wait for every client to close first',
