  q="We create a topic with 2 partitions in a cluster and then open the metadata log. Which new records do we see?"

    'One record called CREATE_TOPIC that holds the whole topic',

  ]}

  q="What is the epoch of the leader of the quorum for?"
  options={[
    'It counts the topics in the cluster',
    'It is the round number of the election. It tells who the current leader is and stops two nodes from both believing that they are the leader',
    'It tells the version of Kafka',
    'It counts the records that producers send'
  ]}
  answer={1}
  why="Every new election raises the epoch. A node that sees a newer epoch knows that the old leader is out of date. The book says this is how a split brain is prevented (book: Kafka: The Definitive Guide, section The Controller, page 136). The exercise saw the epoch rise after the leader was killed (ran)."
/>

</Step>

  q="A cluster of 3 nodes, each both broker and controller, has 2 of them killed. What happens to the command to create a topic?"
  options={[
    'It works as usual, because the broker that is left can take commands',
    'It hangs until it times out, because fewer than half of the controllers are left, so there is no leader that can append to the log',

    'Kafka chooses another controller from what is left'
  ]}
  answer={1}

    'It is kept as a table that is overwritten',

  ]}
