<Question
  client:visible
  lang="en"
  q="Our code reads 6 records, commits at once, and then starts processing. If the process crashes before processing is finished, what is the result?"
  options={[
    'The records that were not processed are read again by the new consumer',
    'The records that were committed but not yet processed are lost, because the new consumer continues from the bookmark',
    'Kafka keeps the records and processes them again automatically',
    'Nothing, because a commit has nothing to do with records'
  ]}
  answer={1}
  why="The bookmark moved forward before the work was done, so the new consumer skips them. The exercise did exactly this and job-0 to job-5 were never processed (ran). Committing after processing risks a repeat but not a loss, which is why it is the more common choice."
/>

</Step>
