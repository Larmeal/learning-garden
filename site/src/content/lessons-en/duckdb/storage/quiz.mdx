  q="How many row groups does a table of one million rows have, and how many rows are in each?"
  options={[
    '1 group of one million rows',
    '9 groups: the first eight have 122,880 rows and the last has 16,960',
    '1,000 groups of 1,000 rows',
    '488 groups of 2,048 rows'
  ]}
  answer={1}
  why="A row group holds at most 122,880 rows (60 vectors). The 2,048 rows are the size of a vector used when processing, not of a row group (ran, source)."

  q="Which of these filters makes the scan look at the fewest rows in an `orders` table whose data is in time order?"
  options={[
    '`customer_id = 5`',
    '`status = \'refunded\'`',
    '`order_ts >= \'2025-12-26\'`',
    'No filter'
  ]}
  answer={2}
  why="The min-to-max ranges of order_ts follow each other without overlap, so only the last group covers 26 December, and the scan looked at 16,960 rows. customer_id and status span the same range in every group, so one million rows had to be looked at (ran)."

</Step>
