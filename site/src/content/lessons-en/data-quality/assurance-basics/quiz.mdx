<Question
  lang="en"
  client:visible
  q="The customers table has 89% usable emails. The marketing team needs at least 98%. The finance team does not use this column at all. Which statement is the most correct?"
  options={['The customers table is low quality and nobody should use it', 'It does not fit marketing, but that does not affect finance. Quality is measured against the needs of each consumer', 'The data passes because it is above 80%']}
  answer={1}
  why="DMBOK2 defines quality as fitness for the consumer's purpose, so the result depends on who uses the data and what they need. The number 89% means nothing until you compare it with the line the consumer accepts, and the 80% line in the last option comes from nobody."
