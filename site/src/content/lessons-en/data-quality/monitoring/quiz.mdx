  q="A detector raises 10 alerts and 4 of them are right. There are 8 real incidents in total. What are the precision and the recall?"
  options={['precision 0.40, recall 0.50', 'precision 0.50, recall 0.40', 'precision 0.80, recall 0.40']}
  answer={0}
  why="precision = TP / (TP + FP) = 4 / 10 = 0.40 (10 alerts, 4 right). recall = TP / (TP + FN) = 4 / 8 = 0.50 (8 real incidents, 4 caught). An easy way to remember: precision asks how much of what you flagged was right, and recall asks how many of the real incidents you caught."
